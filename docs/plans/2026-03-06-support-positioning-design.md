# Support-First Positioning Design

## Goal

Refocus the public narrative of TheNoCodeGuy around human accompaniment: setup, onboarding, training, support, and SAV. The site should stop sounding like a promise of human replacement and instead present AI as a supervised assistant embedded into the client's team.

## Scope

This first pass is intentionally narrow:

- Homepage messaging
- Navigation and footer positioning
- Contact page conversion copy
- Global metadata and page metadata on key acquisition pages
- Critical trust and SEO fixes around legal/privacy links and sitemap behavior

This pass does not attempt a full rewrite of all sector pages or the full `/agent-ia` page.

## Positioning Shift

### Before

- "Hire/recruit an autonomous AI agent"
- "24/7", "never sleeps", "autonomous", "vs employee"
- Productized replacement framing

### After

- "We deploy and accompany a supervised AI assistant"
- "Setup in 48h, training included, human support and SAV"
- Service-led framing with human oversight and operational reliability

## Homepage Changes

The homepage should communicate four things fast:

1. We handle setup
2. We train the team
3. We remain available in support
4. The client keeps control on sensitive actions

The hero rotates through business cases, but each slide should now include the service layer, not just the automation outcome.

The "How it works" section should become a delivery process:

1. Brief and scoping
2. Setup and tool connection
3. Training and first validations
4. Support, SAV, and ongoing adjustments

## Contact Page Changes

The contact page should work as a reassurance page, not just a booking page. It should clearly state:

- We analyze the process with the client
- We handle setup and connection work
- We provide onboarding/training
- We stay reachable after go-live

## Trust and Legal Changes

The current FR legal/privacy pages linked from the footer still contain placeholders. This breaks trust directly.

Preferred action in this pass:

- Fill the FR legal/privacy content with the information already present elsewhere in the repo
- Keep footer links aligned with the completed FR pages
- Update sitemap to surface the preferred FR routes

## SEO Changes

Critical SEO fixes in this pass:

- Replace generic metadata on homepage/contact/blog with support-first messaging
- Fix sitemap so it no longer invents cross-locale blog alternates from FR-only slugs
- Prefer accurate, non-duplicative legal/privacy URLs in sitemap

## Recommendation

This pass should optimize for credibility over breadth. The strongest commercial improvement will come from making the site feel safer and more service-backed, not from adding more pages.
