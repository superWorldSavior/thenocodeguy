# Support-First Positioning Third Pass Design

## Goal

Finish the French-language positioning cleanup so the site no longer mixes a support-led promise with older "autonomous AI staffing" language in secondary pages, pricing copy, workflow pages, legal terms, and selected blog content.

## Scope

This pass covers the remaining FR content that still undermines the new positioning:

- `messages/fr.json` sections for `agents`, `pricing`, `profiles`, `workflows`, and remaining legal wording
- `app/[locale]/workflows/layout.tsx`
- `app/[locale]/cgv/page.tsx`
- FR blog articles that still explicitly push "recruit an AI agent" or employee-replacement framing

This pass stays FR-only. EN/ZH are out of scope.

## Positioning Rules

The remaining copy should follow the same rules as earlier passes:

- Stop framing the offer as "hire/recruit an autonomous agent"
- Stop implying the tool replaces the human relationship
- Keep availability or automation claims only when they describe a capability, not the core value proposition
- Emphasize setup, supervision, team transmission, support, and escalation

## Page Strategy

### Workflows

Workflows should read like deployable operational recipes, not "pilot automatique" promises. Keep the technical specificity, but shift the framing to practical deployment and support.

### Pricing and packaged offer copy

The offer should sound like a managed deployment service with fair use, onboarding, and support, not an autonomous bot sold as labor replacement.

### Blog corrections

Only the FR articles with the strongest mismatch should be edited. The goal is not a full editorial rewrite; it is to remove the most credibility-damaging lines and align the CTA language.

### Legal copy

CGV/privacy/legal text should consistently describe deployment and support services rather than staffing or placement.

## Recommendation

Keep this pass conservative: change wording, not structure. The win here is consistency. Once the entire FR surface tells the same story, the marketing signal becomes much clearer.
